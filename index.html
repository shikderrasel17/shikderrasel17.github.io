I work in data, reporting, and enterprise systems for a living, and I'm trying to get better at research on the side.

Right now: Senior Executive, Sales-MIS at Bashundhara Group in Dhaka — SAP and Power BI during office hours, explainable machine learning in whatever time is left after that.

I studied Management Information Systems, and most of what I do still sits in that space: the systems that hold an organization's data, and the reports and decisions built on top of them. In my day job that means SAP, sales numbers, and reports that go up to people who act on them. Outside of work, I'm slowly building a research habit around machine learning that doesn't just make a prediction but explains itself well enough that someone without a technical background can actually trust it. I don't have all of this figured out yet — this site is here to show what I've actually done, not a highlight reel.
