Industry Work

Enterprise MIS, sales operations, and reporting infrastructure. Case studies below are anonymized where figures are confidential; no proprietary data is shown.

Sales-MIS and SAP operations, industrial group

Context: Multi-entity industrial group (piling, ready-mix concrete, cement), Dhaka.

Problem: Sales target setting, achievement tracking, and price approval preparation ran across SAP and manual spreadsheets, slowing executive reporting and creating version drift between teams.

My role: Own SAP sales-order operations (VA01, BP, MM60, custom Z-reports), sales analytics, and the Monthly Chairman's Report.

Approach: SAP transaction data, Power BI dashboards, Excel and Python for reconciliation and forecasting.

Outcome: Consolidated reporting replaced parallel manual tracking for target-vs-achievement analysis and executive reporting.

Hospital Information System coordination, 13-center eye-care network

Context: 13-center network (3 eye hospitals, 1 maternity hospital, 9 vision centers), Quasem Foundation.

Problem: No standardized KPI reporting across centers; international donor reports (Seva Foundation, Cure Blindness Project, SightSavers) were compiled manually and inconsistently.

My role: End-to-end ERP/HIS coordination across all 13 centers.

Approach: Automated Power BI, Excel, and SQL KPI reporting pipelines; standardized donor report formats; contributed to R&D on the Vision Center service model.

Outcome: Recurring KPI reports and donor reports moved from manual compilation to a standardized, repeatable pipeline across all centers.

Databases for donor-funded programs, non-profit sector

Context: Geist International Foundation — GCERF/Kofi Annan Foundation and U.S. Department of State-funded programs.

Problem: Student and beneficiary tracking across a 500+ participant scholarship program, plus finance and impact data, needed a reliable system for reporting to international funders.

My role: Business Development Intern, then Administrative Operations Manager.

Approach: Built and maintained databases for student tracking, finance, and program impact.

Outcome: Reliable tracking and reporting supported funder relationships across two donor-funded programs.
